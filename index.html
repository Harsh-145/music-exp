🎹 Musical Instruments

Web Harmonium

Play an interactive 3-octave harmonium using your keyboard or MIDI device. Features octave shifting, note stacking, and reverb effects.

💡 Tip: Use Q-P keys to play notes; Z/X to shift octaves

Web Flute

Play Indian classical flute with semitone transposition. Stack multiple notes for harmony and explore different ragas.

💡 Tip: Keyboard shortcuts available; MIDI device supported

Web Tambura

Generate authentic tambura drone sounds. Adjust pitch and volume to create the perfect background for your practice.

💡 Tip: Loop-based drone generation for continuous support
